Privacy Policy for GOONTRACK

100% PRIVATE & LOCAL

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

GOONTRACK is built with a privacy-first, offline-first architecture. We believe your personal habits and session data belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • Zero Remote Servers: GOONTRACK does not operate backend servers or databases.
  • Local Storage Only: All session durations, mood logs, timestamps, notes, streaks, and XP points are saved exclusively inside your device's local sandboxed storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

GOONTRACK contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, NO analytics tracking scripts, and NO data brokering tools. Your telemetry never leaves your device.

4. Data Deletion & Factory Reset

You have complete control over your data. You can permanently wipe all logs, streaks, and XP at any time via the Settings > Danger Zone: Factory Reset menu or by clearing the app data in your Android system settings.

5. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/thamaracode/goontrack.
